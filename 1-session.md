# The first session (planned to be a one shot but then turned into a longer campaign):

The group of a wizard gnome, a bard human, a paladin human, a barbarian dwarf and a rogue dark elf met someone shady in a bar

The shady person gave them a quest to find a lost relic claiming it is family heirloom. Gave a clue about a library

The party checked a library and solved a couple simple puzzles to obtain a map showing the location of heirloom

The party had some adventures on the way

They arrived at a cave where there was a guardian (ilvanyth) of the heirloom and the shady person (varun) came right after the party. Pushing the party into choosing a side between ilvanyth and varun

The part split into two: dark elf, paladin and bard attacked the guardian and barbarian and wizard attacked varun, wizard tried to save ilvanyth

Both varun and ilvanyth died

Paladin and bard reached the heirloom, the heirloom was a prison for an old evil god. The old god promised anything they wish for freedom

Paladin decided to betray his devotion and freed the evil god

bard tried to stop the paladin but got slayed by the paladin

Paladin freed the evil god in exchange for the Bahamut (the platinum dragon) to be his pet (as backstory paladin was previously devoted to Bahamut)
