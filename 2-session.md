# The second session:

The promise gets twisted, and the old evil god Voruzhan only gives an essence of the platinum dragon Bahamut inside a mechanical dragon.  --- not known to PCs

The paladin needs to complete a ritual to capture and enslave Bahamut. --- not known to PCs



Finally, they reach outside and see the paladin riding a chained dragon that looks pale, unlike the true platinum dragon Bahamut. It seems like silver and also not silver, but definitely not platinum
They also see another dragon flying while carrying something that looks glimmerous. This dragon is completely silver, no doubt.
They also see something dark…

As the entrance of the cave is collapsing, Ilvanyth reaches out to the mind of the wizard, showing some images:
1. Voruzhan is wreaking havoc in the world
2. Old times dragons, wizards, and dwarves made an alliance to forge the seal for Voruzhan
3. Wizards and Dwarves created their own seal, and both pieces are forged under a flask of dragon flame
4. The seal is shattered into 2 pieces after use
5. Each faction takes its shard and keeps it in a safe place
6. Wizards keep it in a sacred library hidden behind deadly puzzles
7. Dwarves keep it in a clan mine
First thing to do is to go after the silver dragon to save the bard. The nest is not too far away. Bard travels with the group as a ghost.

As they are travelling, they see the darkness devouring the earth. The sun is covered by black clouds all the time. The whole world is in the dark. You feel things walking in the shadows.



On the way to the dragon, they encounter a couple of tests to see if they have good intentions or not:

 Someone trying to run away filled their horses too much. The horse is sick but the person is still pushing it. --- They killed the person and freed the horse. Dhampir sucked the blood of the person they killed.


```
The mountain path narrows until it ends abruptly at a sheer wall of smooth stone. It stretches across the entire gorge, four meters high, with a single door set into its center. The stone is flawless, without cracks or weathering, as though it had been placed here yesterday. The door has no handle, no hinges — just a flat outline carved into the wall.
The air feels strangely still here, as if the mountain itself is holding its breath. The wall doesn’t quite fit with the rest of the landscape — it’s too perfect, too deliberate.
You notice a cryptic writing in both draconic and common: “The strongest walls are built in the mind.”
```
There is a cemetery on the left and huge walls on the right

Investigation (DC ~15): They notice the wall doesn’t cast a proper shadow, or the door outline doesn’t line up with the stone texture.
Insight (DC ~16): They sense the wall is more of a mental barrier than a physical one.
Physical Interaction: If someone throws a rock, it passes through
Magic:Detect Magic reveals it’s an illusion. Dispel Magic temporarily makes it shimmer and fade.
Arcana check:
	<10: 	This looks like an ordinary wall
	10-12: 	There is something magical about this wall
	13-14:	It does not feel real
	15-17:	Beliefs are what hold people back
	18>:		Reveal the magic
They can approach the wall differently; they can try to lockpick the door, they can try to climb up and jump over the wall, they can try to knock the door down with brute force, or they can try to talk to small animals to unlock it from behind.


They approach the dragon. It looks magical. Dragon’s attitude deeply depends on the party’s previous actions. If they are good, the dragon is kinder and helps. If the party has been naughty, more reluctant. The party can try to intimidate, persuade, sneak, and steal, forcefully steal the divine dragon breath they need for the ritual to stop Voruzhan. They were okayish so the dragon doesnt cause too much trouble. Tells them they need a body for reviving the Bard.



The party decides to go to the graveyard. There is a fight with zombies as they trigger some traps. At the end they dig a body and get it to the dragon.
